# Sales-Dashboard
## Recommended Structure and Order
### 1.	Project Title / Headline
Madhav Ecommerce Sales Dashboard
An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyse ecommerce sales performance across customers, categories, states, payment modes, and time periods.
### 2.	Short Description / Purpose
The Madhav Ecommerce Sales Dashboard provides a clear and interactive view of sales, profit, quantity, and average order value (AOV). It helps stakeholders quickly understand business performance, identify profitable segments, and track trends over time for better data driven decision making.

### 3.	Tech Stack
The dashboard was built using the following tools and technologies:<br>
•	📊 Power BI Desktop – Main data visualization platform used for report creation.<br>
•	📂 Power Query – Data transformation and cleaning layer for reshaping and preparing the data.<br>
•	🧠 DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) – Used for calculated measures, dynamic visuals, and conditional logic.<br>
•	📝 Data Modeling – Relationships established among tables (resorts, snow, and data_dictionary) to enable cross-filtering and aggregation.<br>
•	📁 File Format – .pbix for development and .png for dashboard previews

### 4.	Data Source
Source: Sample ecommerce transactional dataset used for Power BI portfolio projects.
Includes order details, customer data, product categories, state-wise sales, payment modes, and key metrics such as sales, profit, and quantity.
### 5.	Features / Highlights
The best dashboard explanation format. 
•	Business problem
•	Goal of the dashboard
•	Walk through of key visuals (briefly!)
•	Business impact & Insights

Example:
Business Problem
Ecommerce data is often fragmented, making it difficult to track performance, identify top customers and regions, analyse trends, and understand payment behaviour.

•	Goal of the Dashboard
To deliver an interactive dashboard that monitors key ecommerce KPIs, enables filtering by state and quarter, highlights high- and low-performing segments, and supports data-driven business decisions.

📊 Walkthrough of Key Visuals

•	KPIs: Displays total Sales (438K), Profit (37K), Quantity (5,615), and AOV (121K) for a quick performance snapshot.


•	Filters: State and Quarter slicers enable dynamic regional and time-based analysis.

•	Customer & Category Analysis: Identifies top customers and shows Clothing as the highest-volume category (63%).

•	Time Trends: Monthly profit chart highlights seasonal patterns and loss-making periods.

•	Geographic Insights: State-wise sales ranking reveals top-performing regions.

•	Payments & Products: Payment mode distribution and sub-category profit charts uncover customer preferences and high-margin products.

• Business Impact & Insights
This dashboard enables quick tracking of sales, profit, and quantity trends, giving stakeholders clear visibility into overall business performance. It helps identify high-value customers for targeted engagement, highlights top-performing states to support regional marketing and logistics planning, and reveals a strong reliance on Cash on Delivery—indicating opportunities to encourage digital payment adoption. Additionally, category- and sub-category-level insights support better inventory planning and product optimization decisions.
